Reel ideas this week

1. Why your videos look robotic
You read off-camera. Viewers see your eyes drift away. Trust drops. Cut to side-by-side: eye contact vs not. Hook: stop looking like a robot. End: your eyes belong on the lens.

2. The one-take trick
Memorizing scripts wastes hours. Three takes minimum. Then editing. Show clock. Show stack of failed takes. Then SteadyEye. One take. Done.

3. What I learned from 100 Reels
Most creators record five takes or more. Pro creators record one. The difference: they don't memorize. They use prompters. But not the scrolling kind.
